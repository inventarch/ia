# Product structural distribution

The optional `product-system` owns product structure. Its native distribution,
`inventarch/product-structure`, is independently versioned at 0.1.0 and explicitly
selected by consumers. The canonical language and foundation roots do not acquire
it automatically. Its version is separate from the IA 1.0.0 toolchain release.

Consumer qualification installs public DB/distribution code and actual
language/product archives, authors a product, resolves its owner and plans, and
refuses absent adoption, wrong words, dangling references and malformed fields.
Application directory mappings, lifecycle policy and effects belong to consumers.

Workspace describes composition, distribution describes release closure, contract
describes obligations, and plan/milestone/task/decision describe work. None owns
the stable product identity associating plans. No site/workspace-role word is minted.

`consumer.mjs` is the explicit pure structural reader shipped as a hash-pinned
asset. Installation does not execute it or grant it authority. The independent
qualification host explicitly selects it after verifying the complete archive
closure; callers supply an admitted public EditorSnapshot and exact revision.

The native schema enforces number type, closed status enumeration and typed
references. The product consumer additionally enforces format exactly 1, nonempty
title, unique resolvable plan references and a resolvable agent owner. Generic
native dangling-reference warnings are not evidence of product-consumer success.
Retired is authored domain state, not automatic deletion, revocation or lifecycle
execution. API lifecycle policy must qualify separately.

The distribution carries only its own system and teaching assets; foreign system
definitions come from the exact declared language dependency. Instances belong to
consumer-local systems. Extending products requires a separate local owner and
explicit references, not mutation/re-registration of product or patched base schema.

Qualification must record exact source/archive/dependency identities and separate
structural findings from consumer semantics. No customer records, private methods,
provider credentials or grant semantics belong in this release.
