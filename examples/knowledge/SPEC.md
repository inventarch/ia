# Knowledge example

This example binds one public, synthetic native record to a Markdown body. Its committed selector exports exactly that record and body under the installed public native foundation.

Local search and reading require no selector or .kb directory. Export requires an explicit committed selection and full commit identity. Readers preserve exact bodies and snapshot citations; imports remain inert and grant no host authority.

The installed qualification journey covers this contract with equivalent synthetic content in a clean repository. The example does not provision a central store, publish content or certify compliance.
