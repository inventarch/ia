# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a place where anyone can report a problem, propose a change or ask a question and be treated with respect. Participation is open to everyone regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct and factual. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give and accept review in good faith. A rejected change is not a judgement of its author.
- Assume the other person has context you lack, and ask before concluding.
- Respect the time of maintainers and contributors: search before filing, fill in the templates, and keep discussions on topic.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation or discrimination in any form.
- Insults, personal attacks, or sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests or discussions after being asked to stop.

## Scope

This applies in every space this project runs: the repository, its issues, pull requests, discussions and advisories, and anywhere a person is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the maintainers at <admin@inventarch.dev>. If the concern involves that address's owner, report it to another maintainer listed in [CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS).

Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainers will review the report, may ask the reporter for more detail, and will tell the reporter what action was taken.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this document, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone who does. The response is proportionate to the behaviour and its repetition. Someone who disagrees with an enforcement decision may say so in a reply to the maintainers' decision.
